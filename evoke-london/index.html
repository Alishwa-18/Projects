About Evoke

Building Extraordinary
Spaces Since 2013

Evoke Design & Build is a London-based construction company specialising in commercial, residential and hospitality projects. Since 2013 we've delivered exceptional builds across the capital and beyond — always on time, on brief and within budget.

Our experienced team combines deep technical expertise with a client-first approach, ensuring every project — from planning through to handover — is managed with the care and precision it deserves.
